Futbol Shorts

Internal publishing tool for the YouTube channel AIVIRAL.

What it does

  • Generates original football Shorts (trivia, statistics and quizzes) using our own graphics, text animations and narration. No third-party match footage is used.
  • Uploads those videos to our own channel and schedules their publication using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic public statistics (views, likes, comments) of our own videos so we can decide which formats work best.

Who can use it

Futbol Shorts is not a public service. It runs locally on the channel owner's computer and is used by a single user: the owner of the AIVIRAL channel. There is no sign-up and no access for other YouTube users.
